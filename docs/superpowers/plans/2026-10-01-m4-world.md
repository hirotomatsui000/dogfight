# M4 "World" Implementation Plan

**Goal (spec §12.3, §13, §15.4, §20):** take off from a fictional airfield and fight over recognizable
Poland-inspired terrain, day and night, under changing weather, offline and online.

## Decisions made for this milestone

- **Where Lechovia is used:** Dogfight and Free Flight fly over Lechovia by default; the title screen keeps the Test
  Range as a second map. Strike and Training stay on the Test Range, where their layouts and AI balance were tuned.
  The balance tournament also stays on the Test Range.
- **Sky:** the photographed sky cannot show other times of day or other weather, so the game draws a physically
  based sky (three.js `Sky`, Preetham model) with a moving sun, stars and a moon, and lights the scene from it. The
  satellite-photo ground stays.
- **Runway start:** the player chooses AIR or RUNWAY on the title screen; bots always start in the air. The gear
  retracts by itself once the jet is 30 m up, and there is still no landing (spec §23): touching the ground with the
  gear up is a crash.
- **Time:** the start time (Dawn 06:00, Day 12:00, Dusk 18:00, Night 23:00) and whether the clock runs (1 game hour
  per real minute, as in the spec) are chosen on the title screen. Training always flies at noon in clear weather.
- **Weather:** Clear, Scattered, Broken, Overcast and Rain (overcast with light rain).

## Map: Lechovia (shared, deterministic)

- 200 × 200 km, grid 2049² (~97.7 m cells), generated from a seed in `src/shared/data/maps/lechovia/`. The heights and
  a land-cover grid are rasterized (base noise first, then rivers, lakes, the lagoon and airfields are carved or
  flattened into the grid), so generation is O(cells) — a few seconds; the client generates it in a Web Worker while
  the title screen is up, the server once at start.
- Geography: north sea coast with beaches, a sand spit and a lagoon; a northeast lake district of moraine hills and
  ~40 lakes; central plains; a great river from the southern mountains to the sea through the capital, and a second
  river in the west; foothills and a southern range to about 2,400 m; eastern forests and marshes.
- Places: 5 curated cities (Lechów the capital, Morzysko, Odrzyn, Skalnik, Pojezierz), ~60 villages named from
  Polish-like syllables (never a real major city), highways between cities and local roads to villages routed by A*
  over terrain cost (bridges over rivers), and 4 airfields: one per team near the west and east edges of the combat
  area, two neutral.
- Land covers gain `marsh`, `urban` and `airfield`.
- `MapDefinition` changes: `buildHeights()` replaces `height(x, z)`; optional `features` (settlements, roads,
  airfields) used by rendering, the map screen and runway spawns.

## Ground handling (shared)

- `FlightState` gains `gear` (1 down … 0 up) and `onGround`. A runway spawn starts on the team airfield's runway
  threshold at full military power with the gear down; slots line up 200 m apart, alternating sides.
- On the ground: a spring-damper gear contact, rolling friction 0.025 and wheel brakes 0.35 (airbrake key), no side
  slip, nose-wheel steering from the roll and rudder inputs (25 °/s slow, 4 °/s fast), rotation once the dynamic
  pressure gives the elevator authority (up to 14° nose up), lift-off when lift exceeds weight.
- Ground contact exists only inside an airfield's flattened area; leaving it on the ground is a crash. The PULL UP
  warning and the crash rule ignore a jet on its wheels.

## Weather and time (shared)

- `WeatherPreset`: cloud coverage, base and top, haze factor, light factor, rain. `CloudField` is a deterministic
  coverage field (seeded noise) with `densityAt(x, y, z)` and `blocks(a, b)`; clouds block visual detection, IR seeker
  locks and IR missile guidance, never radar.
- `timeOfDay`: hour from the room's start hour and rate; sun and moon directions for latitude 52° N at the equinox.
- Both are room settings: offline from the title screen, online fixed by the room's creator and sent in `welcome`.

## Client

- **Terrain LOD:** a quadtree of 64-cell chunks (levels 0…5 on Lechovia, 0…3 on the Test Range) chosen by distance,
  built in a Web Worker (`?worker&inline`, so the single-file build still works), with skirts against cracks, an LRU
  cache, parents shown until children arrive, and an outer ring beyond the map edge. Replaces the static terrain mesh.
- **Features:** runway, taxiway and apron meshes with markings, hangars and towers; instanced buildings per
  settlement (cities taller and denser); road ribbons near the camera.
- **Sky and light:** the sky dome follows the sun; the environment map is rebuilt when the sun has moved more than
  1.5° or the weather changed; sun and moon lights; stars; haze color and density by sun height and weather; exposure
  for night.
- **Clouds:** cumulus clumps of soft billboards from the shared field (sorted back to front), an overcast deck seen
  from above and below, whiteout fog inside clouds, and rain streaks below the cloud base.
- **Night:** navigation and strobe lights on every jet, city and street lights, runway edge and threshold lights, a
  dimmer HUD.
- **Map screen (M):** the terrain drawn once with rivers, lakes, cities, roads and airfields, the combat area, and
  live markers for the own jet, teammates and contacts.
- **Title screen:** a WORLD row with map, time, clock, weather and start (air or runway) choices; the showcase flies
  over Lechovia under the chosen time and weather.

## Network (protocol 3)

- `hello` carries the map, start hour, clock, weather and the pilot's start choice; `welcome` returns the room's
  settings and the client loads that map before the session starts (messages are buffered meanwhile).
- Aircraft flags gain gear-down and on-ground bits; the own section carries `gear` and `onGround`.

## Tasks

1. **Plan** — this file.
2. **Lechovia generator** — map definition changes, geography, rivers, lakes, lagoon, settlements and names, roads,
   airfields, land cover. Tests: determinism; sea in the north, peaks of about 2,400 m in the south, rivers that fall
   to the sea, lakes in the northeast; airfields flat; villages not in water; roads connected; fictional names.
3. **Ground handling and runway spawns** — physics, World integration, mode opt-in, spawn choice. Tests: every jet
   takes off within the runway at military power and at full afterburner; stays on the centreline; brakes stop it; no
   crash while rolling; leaving the airfield on the ground crashes; gear retracts.
4. **Weather, clouds and time (shared)** — presets, cloud field, detection and seeker integration, sun and moon.
   Tests.
5. **Terrain LOD renderer and worker** — chunk builder, quadtree selection, worker protocol, map loader, material
   classes. Tests for the pure parts.
6. **Airfields, settlements, roads (client)** — meshes, instancing, ribbons.
7. **Sky, lighting, clouds, rain, night lights** — client environment.
8. **Title screen and game wiring** — world options, showcase over Lechovia, runway HUD cue, gear on the models.
9. **Map screen** — M key, drawing, live markers.
10. **Online** — protocol 3, room settings, map loading on welcome. Tests.
11. **Verification and docs** — browser checks (take-off, day, dusk, night, overcast, rain, map screen, online),
    smoke test, spec revision 11, README, handoff, push.
